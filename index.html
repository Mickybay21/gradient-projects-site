How we work

A clear process from first drawing to handover

Every project runs the same way, so you always know what happens next and what it will cost.

  1. Evaluation

    We visit the site and read your drawings and bill of quantities, at no cost and with no obligation.

  2. Priced proposal

    You receive a priced bill with every correction we made to the quantities shown, and a programme.

  3. Construction

    A dedicated project engineer runs the site, with monthly valuations and progress photographs.

  4. Handover

    We walk the building with you, clear the snag list and stand behind the work through the defects liability period.
